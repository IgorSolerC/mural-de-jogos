# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Angular (user-pinned). Standalone components, signals, SCSS. Deploys as static files (GitHub Pages). An optional cloud API lives in `api/` (Hono on Cloudflare Workers with D1, portable to Node + SQLite); without an account the site runs entirely in the browser.

## Users

One person: the owner, keeping a personal diary of the games they have played, the books they have read and the films, series and animes they have watched. Uses it on desktop and on the phone, often right after finishing (or giving up on) something, when the opinion is fresh. Interface language is Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR).

## Product Purpose

A personal review wall ("Meu Mural"). The user finds a game, book, film, series or anime by name, writes a review with scores, and pins it to a wall that grows into a visual record of everything they have played, read and watched. Success: logging a review takes under a minute, and browsing the wall is a pleasure the user comes back to.

## Positioning

Not a social review site and not a backlog tracker. It is a private wall of pinned index cards, one per title, where the collection itself is the artifact. Accounts are optional (Google sign-in, for keeping the wall in sync and sharing it by code); no feed of strangers, no public scores.

## Operating Context

- Reviews are written after a session or after finishing something.
- The wall is browsed to remember, compare, and rank past games, books, films, series and animes.
- Own reviews live only in this browser: in IndexedDB (`meu-mural:dados`), with a mirror copy kept in localStorage while it fits. The first visit after the change copies the localStorage keys, unchanged, into IndexedDB in one transaction and never deletes them; if that copy fails, everything stays on localStorage as before. Each mirror write leaves a fingerprint (`mural-de-jogos:espelho:v1`), so when an older cached version of the site writes to localStorage later (offline), the next visit merges that data in like a backup (newest card wins, deletions stay deleted). See `core/local-data.ts`. The user can export all reviews and import them back as a backup or to move browsers. Backups opened for comparison are kept separately in IndexedDB, persist across visits, and are not included in the owner's backup.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Murais:** five walls: Jogos, Livros, Filmes, Séries and Animes. The header reads "Meu mural de *jogos*"; the word is a button that opens a notepad slip listing the five walls (with counts), and choosing one swaps the whole site to that wall on the same page. The choice is remembered. Nothing crosses walls: each has its own cards, Pra depois queue, Ranking, Lado a lado selection, written bonuses and colour rotation. The backup is one file with every wall. Each wall is a profile in `core/kinds.ts`:

  | Mural | Weighs 2x | Weigh 1x | Status (3) | Amount | Difficulty |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Jogos | Diversão | História, Jogabilidade, Visual | Incompleto / Finalizado / Platinado | hours | yes |
  | Livros | Envolvimento | História, Personagens, Escrita | Larguei / Lido / Relido | pages (whole) | yes, "Dificuldade de leitura" |
  | Filmes | Roteiro, Envolvimento | Atuação, Visual | Não terminei / Assistido / Revisto | — | no |
  | Séries | Roteiro, Envolvimento | Personagens, Visual | Parei / Terminei / Revi | — | no |
  | Animes | Roteiro, Envolvimento | Personagens, Animação | Parei / Terminei / Revi | — | no |

  Stored values never change meaning: a card's `kind` is `jogos`, `livros`, `filmes`, `series` or `animes` (cards from before the walls are `jogos`); the three statuses are stored as `incompleto`, `finalizado`, `platinado` in every wall, only the names change; the same score key means the same thing in every wall (História is História in a game and a book). The field that holds the amount is still `hoursPlayed`, and the reviewed item is still `game`, because renaming them would break older backups.
- **Lookup:** typing a name shows an autocomplete dropdown from the wall's catalogue, all called from the browser (CORS). Books: Open Library, no key; the last word gets a `*` (it only matches whole words), and with `lang=pt` plus the `editions.*` fields (which only come back when `key` is also asked for) each work brings its best Portuguese edition, whose title and cover win ("O Hobbit", "Harry Potter e a Pedra Filosofal"); covers from `covers.openlibrary.org/b/id/{id}-L.jpg?default=false`, so a missing cover errors into "sem capa"; the author is shown under the title and kept on the card. Animes: Kitsu, no key; it completes half words and has the Brazilian name of many titles (`titles.pt_br`, then English, then the canonical one); results whose any name starts with what was typed come first; its images have no CORS, so they show but the service worker cannot keep them. If Kitsu fails, AniList (GraphQL, no key, English or romaji titles). Films and series: TMDB when the user pastes a free key in Ajustes (the short API key goes in the URL, the long read token in an `Authorization: Bearer` header; `language=pt-BR` gives Portuguese names and posters from `image.tmdb.org/t/p/w500`; a 401 says to check the key; Ajustes carries TMDB's attribution line). Without a key, English Wikipedia pages with `Infobox film` or `Infobox television`. Games: Wikipedia (`Infobox video game`) or RAWG, chosen in Ajustes; RAWG needs a key. The Wikipedia autocomplete runs two searches together, the one with the last-word wildcard (for a name still being typed) and one with the words as typed, exact first, because the wildcard alone fills the list with lookalikes ("God of War*" brings Warhammer and Warframe). A trailing year in parentheses (RAWG's "God of War (2018)") is not part of the title when matching; it counts as the year. Cover choices search Wikipedia by exact title, keep the page's year (from the title, "God of War (2018 video game)", or the description), fill in as each source answers, and say when a source failed (a refused RAWG key says to check Ajustes). In the editor, on every wall, "Escolher outra capa" opens the same cover choice as the Wishlist (the item's cover and the others of the same title from the sources below, a pasted https image link, or none), and the live card follows it. Wikipedia needs no key (CORS via `origin=*`, `pageimages` with `pilicense=any` for cover thumbnails). RAWG only offers background art without the title, so when a RAWG game is on Steam (found via RAWG's `/games/{id}/stores`), the cover becomes Steam's official vertical library art (`library_600x900.jpg`); otherwise the RAWG art stays. Rejected after testing: Jikan (MyAnimeList, returned 504), iTunes Search (film search returns nothing), IMDb suggestions (no CORS), OMDb (key, English only), TVmaze (whole words and original titles only), Google Books without a key (the shared anonymous quota was exhausted, 429). The user may also type a name that is not in the list and review it without a cover.
- **Review fields:** the wall's four scores 0–10 (see Murais). By default, the final score (Média) is the weighted average, the wall's centre categories with a base weight of 2x (Diversão in games, Envolvimento in books, Roteiro and Envolvimento in films, series and animes), shown with one decimal. "Dar a nota final na mão" lets the owner replace it with a manual final score from 0 to 11, with one decimal; only this override can reach 11, while categories and the automatic calculation remain within 0–10. "Usar a média" restores the calculated score. Each category has a per-review weight: Relevante (2x its base), Normal, Pouco importante (half), or Não tem (left out of the average and needs no score), so a game is not punished for lacking something it never tried to have (a clicker with no story). Every category that counts needs a score. The wall's amount, optional (hours played, pages read; none for films, series and animes). Status (three, named per wall); Veredito, optional (Masterpiece, Recomendo, Legalzinho, Meh, Chato), each with its own icon; Dificuldade, only on the walls that have it (Nenhuma, Fácil, Média, Complicado, Difícil, Infernal; stored values never change meaning: `dificil` is labelled Complicado, `impossivel` Difícil, and the top level is `infernal`, labelled Infernal); completion date ("Concluído em", or "Jogado até" when Incompleto), today by default and editable for retroactive entries, never in the future. The date is three optional boxes in one strip, Dia / Mês / Ano (the month from a list), with "Hoje" and "Não lembro" shortcuts: all three is an exact day (`AAAA-MM-DD`), month and year is `AAAA-MM`, the year alone is `AAAA`, and all blank is "Data não definida" (always last by date, under its own section). A day needs its month and any part needs the year; a handwritten line under the strip reads back what will be saved ("Só o mês: março de 2024.") or says what is missing. The chosen precision is kept in cards, readers, date sections, sorting and backups, never inventing a day or month; free-text review.
- **Bônus:** optional, as many as the user likes, "a favor" or "contra" (things the four categories do not cover, such as "Trilha sonora incrível" or "Muitos bugs"; each wall has its own ready-made catalog). Each "a favor" bonus pulls the Média as one more score of 10 with weight 1 would (the weight of a Normal História); each "contra" as one more 0 with weight 1. Each bonus moves the Média by at most 0.25 either way (one flaw must not turn a 5.5 into a 4.5): each is measured alone against the categories' average, capped, and the shifts add up; the Média stays within 0–10. Bonuses alone never make a Média: at least one category score is needed. A ready-made catalog per wall (34 for games, about 20 for the others) plus any the user writes; written ones come back as options in later reviews of the same wall (derived from the reviews themselves, so an unused one disappears). Shown as stickers on Completa cards, as a "+N −N" tally on Simples cards, in the reader with the Média they would have without them, in Nota a nota, and in the Ranking's Balanço. The wall search matches bonus names. Included in the JSON backup.
- **Wall:** all reviews as cards, Completas (adds hours, the four scores in one row, and a two-line excerpt; release year lives in the reader) or Simples (cover, name, date, Média, status and verdict only), or Capas (only the cover and the name, to see as many cards as possible: eight per row on a wide screen, three on phones; the status band is left off the photo). Both show the difficulty skulls (filled ones only, no label) beside the date on the same line; Nenhuma shows nothing, a remembered preference. The wall is split into labelled sections that follow the sort (month, grade band, letter or status). Text search. A "Filtrar" tab opens a sticker-sheet of filters, any within a group and all across groups, each option with the count it would show: verdict (or none), status, review text written or blank, decorated or plain card, Média band, difficulty (walls that have it) and year of completion (or no date). Active filters show as removable stickers under the ruler. Sort by completion date, highest score (per category: Média, História, Diversão, Jogabilidade, Visual), alphabetical, and completion status.
- **Rejogadas** (Releituras in Livros, Reassistidas in Filmes, Séries and Animes): the same work again, on its own card. The reader has "Escrever rejogada", which opens the editor in a revisit mode: the item comes from the original and cannot be swapped, scores, status and text start blank, the date starts today, and "Manter notas" copies the original's four scores, weights, bonuses, hand-given final, verdict, difficulty and status (not the date, amount or text). There is no "Salvar pra depois" for a revisit. A revisit stores `revisitOf` (the original's id; a revisit of a revisit points to the same original) and is otherwise an ordinary review: a new revisit starts with the original's cartolina exactly (colour, paper, pattern, scribble, damage, stain, decoration and their draws; the paper art of a revisit is drawn from the original's id, `artIdOf`, so the same kit comes out identical), and the kit can still be changed; it goes in the backup. On the wall it is an independent card, sorted, grouped, searched and filtered like any other, with "↻ Rejogada" before its date (only the icon on Simples; a small ink seal on the cover on Capas); the original shows "↻ 3×" after its date, and section summaries count them apart ("1 jogo · 1 rejogada"). Opening any of them shows a page strip at the top of the reader, "‹ Original · 1 de 3 ›" / "Rejogada 2", with arrows (and ← / →) to flip between the times in order: the original, then the revisits by date (undated last). Revisits stay out of everything that counts each work once: Ranking (except the total amount, which includes them), Lado a lado (not markable), the Extras games, Comparar, the wall switcher counts and every score-based statistic. They do count where time is measured: Estatísticas' Tempo page (per year, month, weekday, hour, pace, streaks, gaps), the total amount (with "45 h em rejogadas" under it) and the words written. Changing the original's item (name, cover) carries over to its revisits; removing the original removes its revisits too (the confirmation says so, and Desfazer brings all back); removing a revisit leaves the original.
- **Pra resenhar depois:** from the editor, a title can be saved with only its name and cover ("Salvar pra depois"), outside the wall. These pending games live on their own page ("Pra depois"), as sheets torn from a notebook, a binder, graph paper, a message pad or a post-it, stuck to the wall (not cartolina), and are not counted, filtered or sorted with reviews. Each sheet has the day it was saved written in its date header, and it yellows with time (from a week, and more after a month), so what has waited longest stands out. A text search filters the queue by name; it can be ordered by newest, oldest or A–Z. Its own add button ("Guardar jogo", a sheet torn from a spiral notebook, in the header where "Nova resenha" is on the other tabs; N opens it too) opens the same dialog as the Wishlist, in a Pra depois mode: only the name and a cover choice, with the live sheet beside it and "Outra folha" to re-draw it; a title already in the queue is not added twice ("Ver na fila"), and one on the wishlist leaves the wishlist when it goes to the queue. Opening one continues the review; pinning it removes it from the queue. Included in the JSON backup.
- **Wishlist:** each wall has its own list of what the user wants to play, read or watch, on its own page ("Wishlist"). Its own add button (a catalogue coupon, "Adicionar jogo", in the header where "Nova resenha" is on the other tabs; N opens it too) opens a dialog with only the name (the wall's autocomplete, or a typed name without cover) and a cover choice: the item's own cover plus others of the same title (games: Wikipedia in English and Portuguese, and with a RAWG key the Steam vertical cover plus RAWG's background art, extra art and screenshots of the same title, the closest year first; films and series with TMDB: the title's other posters, Portuguese first; books: the covers of the work's editions, Portuguese first; animes: Kitsu and AniList; and the Portuguese Wikipedia page of the same name), a pasted https image link, or none. A title already on the list is not added twice ("Ver na lista"); one already reviewed says so and can still be added. Items are magazine clippings glued to the wall as a collage (not cartolina, not notebook pages), each cut its own way (torn by hand, scissors, pinking shears, a perforated insert, sometimes a folded corner) from its own kind of magazine, with the name glued on top as a handwritten cream or kraft strip, a printed headline, a colour band or a ransom note (no year). In the add dialog, "Outro recorte" re-draws the clipping before it is saved. Each wish has a relevance: MUST PLAY (MUST READ, MUST WATCH on the other walls), Comum or LATER, chosen in the add dialog or changed later from the sticker on the clipping (MUST and LATER show one; Comum does not); the list always sorts by it first. A text search filters the list; it can be ordered by newest, oldest or A–Z, and "Sortear" picks one at random ("Que tal jogar Hades?"), with "Começar a resenha" and "Outro". Tapping a clipping starts the review with name and cover filled; pinning it removes it from the list, and "Salvar pra depois" moves it to the Pra depois queue (same id). The editor offers "Tirar da wishlist" (with Desfazer). Included in the JSON backup; removals are remembered like the others.
- **Lado a lado:** on the Mural, a "Marcar" mode lets the user tick any reviews (one selection at a time, kept in this browser, not in the backup) and open them on their own page: only those cards, in one flow, plus a "Nota a nota" sheet comparing every score and fact column by column, with the best of each score row circled. Built for comparing a series (every GTA) or any hand-picked set.
- **Comparar:** opens a colleague's Meu Mural backup (.json or gzip .json.gz, dropped on a kraft envelope or chosen) locally; nothing is imported into the owner's ReviewStore. Pending items and wishlist entries are not evaluations and never enter it. Multiple named colleagues persist separately in IndexedDB (the chosen one is remembered); the name is the one saved inside the backup (Ajustes › Backup › Seu nome), else guessed from the file name ("backup-da-marina.json.gz" → Marina), else asked on the spot. The header shows two "OLÁ, EU SOU" name tags (Você in black, the colleague in blue: the colleague's pen is blue everywhere on this page), other colleagues as small tags to switch, and actions to see the colleague's wall, update the backup (same name, new file), add another colleague or remove one (with Desfazer). The comparison follows the wall open in the header, with shortcuts to other walls that have something to compare. A spiral "caderno de perguntas" answers, for each person: "Vocês combinam?" (affinity from the mean score distance of works in common, on a school ruler, with the biggest disagreement and the highest agreement as notes that jump to that pair), top 3, finish rate, most used bonuses, a dot strip of every final score with the averages, cartolina colours as proportional strips, verdicts counted in tally marks, and total hours or pages. Below, three divider tabs: "Em comum" (each pair: the owner's real card on the left, the colleague's on the right, a note with the difference in the middle and an optional "Nota a nota"), "Dicas de <colega>" (works only the colleague reviewed, each with "Quero jogar/ler/ver" to add it to the owner's wishlist) and "Suas dicas". Matching prioritizes catalogue identity within the same wall, then title with known author/year; ambiguous homonyms are left out of pairs and of both tip lists. The owner's cards open the normal reader; the colleague's open a read-only reader with their name.
- **Mural do colega** (`/comparar/mural`): only the colleague's cards of the open wall, in the same sections as the owner's wall, with search, the same filters, ordering and density, and nothing of the owner's.
- **Extras:** a tab with what is not the wall itself, as cards pinned on a board in two shelves: "Números" (Ranking and Comparar, which used to be tabs, and Estatísticas) and "Jogos" (the Mata-mata; the planned quizzes, such as "Adivinhe pela nota", go here). Every game plays with the wall chosen in `core/players.ts`: the owner's or a colleague's backup opened in Comparar, always only the wall open in the header, read-only.
- **Mata-mata** (`/extras/mata-mata`): a knockout tournament. The rules sheet asks whose wall, how many duels (5, 15, 30 or Todos; N duels are N + 1 cards drawn at random, all cards for Todos) and whether the scores are hidden in the duels (default) or shown. The bracket is a power of two; when the cards do not fill it, exactly the highest-scored cards skip the first round (the rules sheet says how many), so N cards always make N − 1 duels. Every round is drawn when the previous one ends (`pairUp` and `ensureDraws` in `core/tournament.ts`): the cards that went through are paired so every duel has at least 2 points between the two Médias (as shown), drawn at random among the pairings that allow it, so a very good card is not knocked out early by a similar one; when no pairing reaches 2 points (all the scores are close), the pairing with the widest possible gap in its tightest duel is used (sorted by score, the 1st meets the middle one), and with the scores shown the duel says so. An Undo that changes who went through redraws the next round. Each duel shows two cards with a VS sticker, the round name (Oitavas de final, Quartas…) and "Duelo 3 de 15"; tapping a card (or ← / →) sends it on, Desfazer takes back the last pick. The tournament is saved in this browser, one per wall, and continues after leaving. At the end: the champion with a gold ribbon, the runner-up and the semifinalists, how often the pick had the higher score (with a colleague's wall: how often you guessed which one they rated higher), and the whole bracket. "Jogar de novo" redraws with the same rules.
- **Maior ou menor** (`/extras/maior-ou-menor`): one card shows its value, the next is hidden (scores masked), and the player says whether the hidden value is higher or lower (buttons or ↑ / ↓). The rules sheet asks whose wall and what to compare: the Média, one of the wall's four scores (as shown by Ajustes › Nota; a category marked Não tem is left out) or the amount (hours, pages), where the wall has one; cards without that value are left out. A tie counts as right, but the next card is drawn among unseen cards with a different value whenever there is one. Each right guess reveals the value on a price tag, adds to the streak and, after a moment, slides the card over; the first wrong guess ends the game, and seeing every card without a mistake clears the wall. The end shows the streak, the record (kept in this browser per wall, owner and measure, not in the backup) and the whole sequence. The shared pieces of the games (the "Mural de quem?" picker in `ui/player-picker.ts`, the rules sheet and scoreboard styles in `pages/_extras-kit.scss`, records in `core/game-records.ts`) are meant for the next quizzes.
- **Muraldle** (`/extras/muraldle`): the wall's own "Wordle", like Loldle. A secret card per day (per wall and owner, drawn from the date and kept for the day, avoiding the last two weeks' secrets) or as many as wanted in Treino. The player types the name of another card of the same wall (suggestions without accents, keyboard or tap) and each guess gets a row of paper tiles comparing it with the secret: Média, Status, Veredito, Dificuldade (walls with skulls), Ano, Horas or Páginas (walls with an amount), Bônus positivos and Bônus negativos (how many of each) and Cartolina. Green is equal, yellow is close (half a point of Média, one year, one difficulty level, within 20% of the amount, one bonus more or less, the same cartolina colour in the other shade), red is different, grey is missing data; on numbers an arrow points to the secret's side. Every 3 misses unlock one more hint (7 in all, opened in order with "Ver dica", shown as seven dots): 1, the cover blurred in black and white; 2, the first 3 words of the review; 3, 3 more words; 4, the secret's cartolina (paper, pattern, scribble, damage, stain and decoration, without scores or bonuses; the name and the date line show as placeholders in faint ink, "Nome do jogo" and "Data · Horas · Dificuldade" (only the parts the wall has), and the score and verdict label in its secret look, "?" and "Segredo": `ReviewCard`'s `bare` input), and from then on the cover and the words already shown appear inside it, in their usual places; 5, colour on the cover (still blurred); 6, the whole review; 7, the cover without the blur. The secret's name is blanked out of the review text. Winning or giving up shows the secret card and copies a spoiler-free result (only the coloured squares) to paste in a group. The daily game counts a streak of days, the best streak and how many were solved, kept in this browser (`core/muraldle-save.ts`), not in the backup. The table scrolls sideways on phones with the name column fixed.
- **Estatísticas** (`/extras/estatisticas`): the wall in numbers, for the owner or a colleague opened in Comparar (the same "Mural de quem" choice as the games, shown only when there is a colleague), for every year or one year of completion ("Quando", a select tab). Seven pages, each a binder divider tab on the ruler and kept in the address (`?aba=notas`, so Back returns to the previous page), with "1 de 7" and a next-page button at the foot: **Resumo** (six big numbers on pinned cartolinas: count with Pra depois and Wishlist, Média and median, finish rate, total hours or pages or, without an amount, the 10s, the 9+ and the words written; "Quem é você no mural", a portrait in sentences drawn from the numbers; records; top 5 and the bottom 5; Na fila: the oldest pending item, the wishlist by relevance and how long it would take at the last 12 months' pace); **Notas** (histogram of Médias by whole number with the mean and median as pen marks; "A sua régua", a box plot on a school ruler with a slider "Onde fica um 8?" that says what share of the wall is below it; each category's average, median, highest, lowest, 10s and how much it moves with the Média; the weights chosen per category; how much bonuses and hand-given finals move the Média); **Tempo** (per year, or per month inside a chosen year, with the average under each column; a calendar table of years × months shaded by count; weekday; hour the card was pinned; the average over time; busiest month and year, pace, consecutive months, longest gap, cards pinned from memory, date precision); **Hábitos** (finish rate and status table, difficulty with skulls, amount histogram and an amount × Média scatter on a log scale whose dots open the card, release decades and how many years after release things are finished, what is written); **Vereditos e bônus** (verdicts in tally marks with the average and the range of each, cards whose verdict and Média disagree, the most used bonuses on each side with the average of their cards); **Cartolinas** (stocks as strips of their own colour with the average, the "lucky" stock, decorations, the whole kit used, decorated versus plain, cover sources); **Curiosidades** (franchises found by base name or the first two meaningful words, odd facts, A–Z initials, authors, and the owner's Extras records). Every card mentioned opens: the owner's in the normal reader, a colleague's read-only. Counts live in `core/stats.ts` (pure, with tests); a card without the needed data (date, hours, verdict) only leaves the count that needs it.
- **Ranking:** a separate page ranks the open wall's reviews by Média or by one of its categories, with totals (count, the three statuses, the amount and the longest, most given verdict, overall average).
- **Pages:** five tabs in the shared header: Mural, Pra depois, Wishlist, Extras and Ajustes (Ajustes is a small tape with only a gear, named for screen readers and in its tooltip). Ranking and Comparar live inside Extras, and the Extras tab stays lit on them. From 1230px the tabs sit in one row between the cartaz and the add button (tighter under 1480px); below that they drop to their own row, and on phones all five share one row.
- Reviews can be edited and deleted. Deletions are remembered (and travel in the backup), so merging an older backup never brings a deleted review or pending item back.
- Must work offline for existing reviews: a service worker (`public/sw.js`, production only) keeps the site and every cover already seen; the site is installable (manifest). Lookup needs a network connection and must fail gracefully without one.
- Seu nome: Ajustes › Backup has a "Seu nome" field (up to 40 characters, a preference kept in this browser). The backup carries it as `owner.name` (same version 2; older versions ignore the field) and in the file name, "meu-mural-de-igor-2026-10-05.json.gz" (without a name, "meu-mural-2026-10-05.json.gz"), with a preview under the field. Comparar uses it for the colleague's name; restoring one's own backup in a browser with no name fills the field.
- Cloud (optional): signed in with Google, the wall syncs between devices through `api/` (revision numbers, merge with the backup rules, no lost edits when two devices change it); the first time on a device that already has a wall, the user chooses to merge or keep only the account's, and the previous wall is kept for 30 days. Without an account, nothing changes.
- Sharing by code: each account has a code (K7QF-M2XA) and a link (`?mural=`); anyone, signed in or not, opens that person's public wall (reviews and name only) in Comparar, alongside walls loaded from backup files. The code can be changed in Ajustes › Conta.
- Seguir e Amigos (with the cloud on and an account): follow someone by code (typed in Amigos › Pessoas, or "Seguir" on their wall opened by code). Following is one-way, without approval; the person gets one "começou a seguir você" note (never again for the same pair, even after unfollowing and following again). From then on, every review they pin (sent with the sync as "novas": created in the last 7 days, at most 10 per sync and 30 per day) reaches the follower's Amigos page (`#/amigos`, a tab "Amigos" with a people icon beside the gear, a yellow count of unseen items, and a one-time wave of the icon when something arrives; the word hides on phones). Reviews from before the follow never arrive. Amigos checks the cloud when the site opens and every 15 minutes with the tab visible (204 when nothing is new), keeps what arrived in this browser, shows each review of the last 30 days on its own as the friend's real wall card (from their public wall, downloaded only when the page opens, at most 12 people, with "mudou?"), with a note beside it: "{nome} deu +1,4 que você" and your score when you reviewed the same work, otherwise "Quero jogar" to the wishlist; the name opens their wall. "Evitar spoilers dos amigos" (on by default) masks the card and its reader for works you have not reviewed. "Misturado" (default) shows every wall's news; "Separado" only the wall open on the cartaz, and the tab's number follows it (seen-until is one timestamp for all walls, so opening Amigos in separated mode marks up to the newest item of that wall). Opening it marks everything seen on every device. Muting someone keeps them on the page without counting; unfollowing and removing a follower are there too, and the number on the tab can be turned off (Ajustes › Mural, with the other friend options). Who you follow is kept with the feed in this browser, so a reload shows the right buttons before the cloud answers; changes apply as soon as the cloud confirms them. Limits: 300 people followed, 60 new follows per day. Rotating your code does not drop followers (follows keep the account id); removing a follower does.
- Convite para entrar: with the cloud on, nobody signed in and something on the wall, the notice strip shows "O seu mural só existe neste navegador. Entre com o Google para guardar na nuvem e abrir em qualquer aparelho." with Google's own sign-in button (signing in right there; GIS only allows its button to open the sign-in) and an X. It appears at most once every 2 days: showing it pushes the next time 2 days ahead, and closing does too (`meu-mural:lembrete-conta`). It stays until closed while the page is open, never shows in Ajustes (the button is in Perfil), and replaces the backup reminder when both are due.
- Chaves sincronizadas: signed in, the RAWG and TMDB keys travel in the private cloud wall (`chaves: { rawg, tmdb, em }`), never in the backup file or the public wall. Typing, pasting or clearing a key stamps `keysAt`; the newest stamp wins on every device (clearing included), and keys from before the stamp existed lose to keys already in the cloud. SYNC_SCHEMA went to 2 so an old cached site stops instead of dropping the field.
- Esperando a nuvem: every button whose action waits for a response shows a spinning pen ring in place of its icon (`[appBusy]`, `ui/busy.ts`, `.carregando`), gets `aria-busy`, refuses more clicks, and usually says what it is doing ("Seguindo…", "Saindo…", "Abrindo o mural…"); the other actions of the same row wait. Reduced motion shows a still ring.
- Novidades: `#/novidades` lists what changed in each update, newest first (`core/news.ts`, written in the same commit as the change, bundled with the site: no API calls, works offline). It opens from Ajustes ("Novidades", with how many are unseen). An update may carry a one-line notice for a banner above the wall, with "Ver o que mudou" and an X; closing it or opening the page hides it for good (kept in this browser, `meu-mural:novidades`), and it may have an end date. Only the newest update can show a banner. A first visit with an empty wall counts every update as seen; a browser that already had a wall when the page arrived sees only the newest one. An urgent notice can also come from `public/cloud.json` (`aviso`: id, texto, ate), even with the cloud off; it comes first and has no link. "Apagar o save" keeps what was seen.
- Security: walls from other people (code, link or file) are untrusted. Every review goes through `sanitizeReview` (ids, kinds, statuses, verdicts, paper fields and seeds must be known values or numbers; covers must be https; text is length-capped), gzip is capped while it is unpacked, and text is only ever rendered by Angular interpolation; the only `innerHTML` is the app's own SVG art, built from those checked values. `src/index.html` carries a Content Security Policy: scripts only from the site and Google's sign-in, network only to the site, the cloud API, Google's sign-in and the lookup services (Wikipedia, RAWG, Open Library, Kitsu, AniList, TMDB), images from any https. A new lookup service or a new API address must be added to `connect-src` in the same commit. Critical-CSS inlining is off in production because its `onload` handler would be blocked.
- Data safety: pinning a card asks the browser for persistent storage; a notepad reminder above the wall asks for a backup when the last one is over 30 days old and something changed since (or, with no backup ever, from 5 cards); "Depois" snoozes it for a week.

## Brand Commitments

- The wall is a literal corkboard/mural: reviews look like physical cards, each slightly tilted at a random angle and held to the wall by a pin. (User-pinned, binding.)
- "LINDO, mas sem sacrificar usabilidade": beauty is a requirement, but it never gets in the way of reading, filtering, or editing.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing reviews, logos, or assets. Cover art comes from the lookup source at runtime. Example reviews used during development are synthetic and must not ship as defaults.

## Product Principles

1. The wall is the product. Every review earns its place as a physical-feeling object.
2. Fast to log. Finding the game and scoring it should feel like jotting on a card, not filling in a form.
3. Private and portable. Without an account, nothing leaves the browser except lookup queries. With one, the wall goes to the user's own cloud copy and nothing else does (no e-mail stored). Either way the user can always take their data with them.
4. Delight never blocks the task. Tilts, pins, and textures stay out of the way of reading, searching, and sorting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable autocomplete (combobox pattern), readable contrast on the wall texture, respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, usable at 360px width.
