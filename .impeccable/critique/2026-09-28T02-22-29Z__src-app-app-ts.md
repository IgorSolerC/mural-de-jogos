## Priority Issues
- **[P1] Verdict inks share hues with stocks.** Recomendo 151° = verde 150°, Meh 66° ≈ laranja 60°, Chato 302° = lilás 302°, Legalzinho 264° ≈ azul 234°: tone-on-tone mud on one stock, vibrating complement on another (green on rosa 1.9:1). Fix: one black ticket ink; hue only as a lightened icon. → shipped.
- **[P1] Gold foil dissolves on laranja/lilás (1.04–1.05:1).** Fix: gold foil lettering on the black ticket. → shipped.
- **[P1] Stocks unequal in L/C (rosa L .71 C .20, amarelo L .93); rotation places complements adjacent.** Fix: OKLCH retune L .78–.90; rotation rosa→laranja→amarelo→verde→azul→lilas. → shipped.
- **[P2] Faded section tabs muddy and failing contrast (3.7–4.2:1, counts ~2.5:1).** Fix: Dymo embossed tape, yellow for current. → shipped.
- **[P2] Pins from six hues independent of stock.** Fix: red/white only, white on rosa/laranja. → shipped.
- **[P2] Pastel sheets (stock mixed into paper) look clinical.** Fix: index-card paper with stock header band and red rule. → shipped.
- **[P3] Cool wall/plastic vs warm tape/receipts; editor digits noisy.** Fix: warm neutrals; unselected digits at 60% ink. → shipped.
